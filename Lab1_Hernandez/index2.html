About the Organization

Welcome to the Student IT Association (SITA), the premier student-led community dedicated to advancing software development, cybersecurity, and modern web technologies. Our mission is to empower students through hands-on workshops, hackathons, and collaborative tech projects.

Requirements to Join

  • Must be a currently enrolled undergraduate student in any IT, Computer Science, or Engineering program.
  • Maintain good academic standing with a strong passion for learning technology.
  • Attend the semester orientation and complete the membership registration form below.
